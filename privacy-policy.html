Privacy Policy for IPO Lens

Effective date: September 5, 2026

IPO Lens ("IPO Lens", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the IPO Lens Android application.

IPO Lens is designed to provide informational and educational content about Initial Public Offerings (IPOs). IPO Lens does not provide investment advice, brokerage services, or personalized investment recommendations.

1. Information We May Collect

Account Information

You may use parts of IPO Lens as a guest. If you choose to sign in using Google, authentication is handled using Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication.

Depending on the information made available through your Google account, we may receive basic account information such as:

  • Your Google account identifier
  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Profile image, if made available

IPO Lens does not receive or store your Google password.

Watchlists, Preferences and Reminders

If you use account synchronization, IPO Lens may store information associated with your account including:

  • IPOs saved to your watchlist
  • Notification and reminder preferences
  • Application preferences that are synchronized across devices
  • Saved research or comparison information, where supported

Push Notification Information

IPO Lens may use Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver IPO-related notifications and reminders. A device registration token may be processed and associated with your account or device so that notifications can be delivered.

Notification content may relate to IPO events such as bidding dates, allotment updates, prospectus publication, listing dates, or other alerts you have chosen to receive.

Subscription and Purchase Information

IPO Lens Pro subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. IPO Lens does not directly collect or store your payment card details.

We may process information required to verify and manage your subscription, such as a Google Play purchase token, subscription product identifier, subscription status, and entitlement status.

Advertising and Consent Information

The Free version of IPO Lens may display advertisements through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising technologies may process device, advertising, consent, and usage information according to their own policies.

Where required, IPO Lens uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain or manage advertising consent choices.

IPO Lens Pro is intended to provide an ad-free experience while an active Pro entitlement is recognized.

Technical and Diagnostic Information

IPO Lens may process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the application. Depending on enabled services, this may include:

  • App version
  • Device and operating system information
  • Error and crash diagnostics
  • Network or service failure information
  • Performance information

If Firebase Crashlytics or similar diagnostic services are enabled in the production application, those services may process diagnostic information according to their applicable privacy terms.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

IPO Lens stores certain information locally to improve performance and allow faster or offline access. This may include:

  • Cached IPO information
  • Watchlist information
  • Application preferences
  • Theme settings
  • Notification preferences
  • Recent application state

Local data may be stored using Android technologies such as Room, DataStore, or other application storage mechanisms.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide and operate IPO Lens
  • Authenticate users
  • Synchronize watchlists and preferences
  • Deliver notifications and reminders requested by users
  • Verify IPO Lens Pro subscriptions
  • Display advertisements to eligible Free users
  • Remember consent and application preferences
  • Improve application reliability, security, and performance
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, or unauthorized access

4. IPO and Market Information

IPO Lens displays IPO, company, financial, subscription, prospectus, exchange, and related market information obtained from third-party and/or official sources.

This market information is generally public or commercially sourced information and is not treated as personal user information.

IPO Lens may cache such information locally or on its backend systems for performance, synchronization, analysis, and display.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

IPO Lens may use third-party services to provide application functionality. These may include:

  • Google Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Firebase Crashlytics, if enabled
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform
  • Cloud hosting and backend infrastructure providers
  • IPO and financial-data providers

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual obligations.

6. Data Sharing

IPO Lens does not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers only where reasonably necessary to operate features such as authentication, notifications, subscriptions, advertising, diagnostics, cloud infrastructure, or security.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, legal process, security requirements, or to protect the rights and safety of IPO Lens, its users, or others.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information handled by IPO Lens.

Network communication with production services is intended to use encrypted HTTPS connections. Authentication and subscription verification are handled through appropriate backend and third-party service mechanisms.

No electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and therefore we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account functionality, meet legitimate security or operational needs, or satisfy applicable legal requirements.

Locally cached IPO information may remain on your device until it is refreshed, removed by the application, application data is cleared, or the application is uninstalled.

Certain records may need to be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, financial, legal, accounting, or regulatory purposes.

9. Account and Data Deletion

If you have created or signed in to an IPO Lens account, you may request deletion of your IPO Lens account and associated user data.

Account deletion is available through the application where supported and through our external deletion page:

Account & Data Deletion

Deleting your IPO Lens account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Google Play subscriptions should be managed or cancelled through Google Play.

Some information may be retained where necessary for legitimate security, fraud prevention, billing, accounting, legal, or regulatory purposes.

10. Notification Choices

You can control IPO notification preferences within IPO Lens and can disable application notifications through Android system settings.

11. Advertising Choices

Where available, advertising consent and privacy choices may be managed through the Privacy & Ads section within IPO Lens and through mechanisms provided by Google or your device.

12. Children's Privacy

IPO Lens is not specifically designed for children. The application provides financial-market informational and research content.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

13. International Processing

Third-party service providers and cloud infrastructure used by IPO Lens may process information in countries other than the country where you reside. Such processing is subject to applicable provider safeguards and legal requirements.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to IPO Lens, legal requirements, or service providers.

The effective date displayed at the top of this page will indicate the most recent published version.
